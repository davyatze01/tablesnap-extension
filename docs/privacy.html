TableSnap

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Applies to the TableSnap extension for Chrome and Microsoft Edge and to this website.

Summary: TableSnap works 100% locally in your browser. It does not collect, sell, share or transmit the content of the pages you visit or the tables you export. It has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies. The only network request is license activation/validation with LemonSqueezy, made only if you buy and activate TableSnap Pro.

1. Data controller

Davide Atzeni (“we”), developer of TableSnap. Contact: GitHub Issues of the tablesnap-extension repository.

2. What the extension processes, and where

DataPurposeWhere it stays
Tables and lists of the page you are viewingDetect, preview and convert them to CSV / Markdown / TSV / XLSX / JSON when you click the TableSnap iconOnly in your browser's memory; the files are generated locally and saved to your device or clipboard by you

TableSnap reads a page only after you click its icon (Chrome's activeTab permission) and only that tab. It does not run in the background on the sites you visit and does not access your browsing history.

3. LemonSqueezy (Pro licenses and payments)

Purchases are processed by LemonSqueezy, which acts as merchant of record and handles payment, invoicing and VAT. Your payment and billing data are provided directly to LemonSqueezy under its privacy policy; we receive the order details needed to manage sales (e.g. name, e-mail, country, amount).

When you activate Pro, the extension sends to api.lemonsqueezy.com: the license key and a random instance name (e.g. “TableSnap Chrome a1b2c3”). About every 7 days it re-validates the key by sending the license key and instance ID. No page content is ever sent. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

4. What we do not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels or fingerprinting.
  • No sale or transfer of data to third parties; no use for advertising, credit-worthiness or lending.
  • No remote code: all code ships inside the extension package.

The use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you remove it: use “Reset site preset” / “Remove license” in the popup, or uninstall the extension (which deletes all of its local storage). Order records are kept by LemonSqueezy and by us for as long as required by tax law.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your personal data and to object to its processing, by contacting us through the channel above (please don't post personal data in a public issue: ask for a private contact instead). You can also lodge a complaint with your data-protection supervisory authority (in Italy: Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it).

7. This website

This site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no analytics. GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses in server logs for security purposes (GitHub privacy statement).

8. Children

TableSnap is not directed to children under 16 and does not knowingly process their data.

9. Changes

We will publish any change on this page and update the date above.
